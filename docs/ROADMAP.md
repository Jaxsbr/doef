# Potential future work

These are product opportunities, not a committed sequence or execution log.

## Strong near-term candidates

1. **Safe, versioned `.doef` imports** — validate every field, introduce a schema
   version and migrations, prevent ID collisions, and show actionable errors for
   invalid files.
2. **Complete sheet-library management** — create, duplicate, rename, and delete
   sheets with clear confirmation and collision behavior.
3. **Dynamics and ornaments** — model, render, play, save, and import accents,
   ghost notes, flams, drags, and rolls.
4. **Flexible subdivisions** — support eighth notes, sixteenth notes, and triplets
   while keeping count labels, playback timing, storage, and printing consistent.
5. **Focused practice playback** — loop a selected bar range and optionally ramp
   the tempo after successful repetitions.

## Further possibilities

- A built-in rudiment and groove template library
- MIDI export and higher-fidelity PDF export
- Shareable URL-encoded sheets without a backend
- Touch-first editing and accessibility improvements
- Practice history and progress tracking
- Optional custom percussion samples
